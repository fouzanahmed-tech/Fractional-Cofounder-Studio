About the studio

Built for founders who need someone in the work — not just on the call.

Fractional Cofounder Studio exists because most early-stage founders do not need more strategy documents. They need a capable operator working beside them on the actual problem.

The problem we solve

One unresolved bottleneck can stall a whole company.

Idea-stage and pre-seed founders carry too many company functions at once. Sales goes un-worked because hiring feels more urgent. Hiring stalls because there is no digital presence to point candidates toward. Digital presence stays broken because the founder is chasing product.

The result is a company moving in circles. The fix is not a consultant's report — it is someone who can pick the right bottleneck and actually do the work to unblock it.

What makes us different

  • Execution over advice We do not hand you a deck and leave. We work inside the problem alongside you.
  • One bottleneck at a time Focused work on the real constraint — not a sprawling retainer with no clear output.
  • Transfer built in Every sprint ends with a process, system, hire, or playbook the company keeps.
  • No long commitments to start The first step is always a free bottleneck diagnosis. No pitch deck required.
  • Flexible models Monthly, project, revenue share, or selective equity — structured around how deep we go.

Who runs the studio

A founder operator, not an advisor.

Fractional Cofounder Studio is led by an operator who has sat in the founder seat — working across sales, marketing, hiring, product, operations, and fundraising across early-stage companies.

The perspective is practical: what does this specific company need to unblock in the next 2–6 weeks, and how do we actually get there?
