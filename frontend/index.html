Common questions

What founders ask before starting.

What makes this different from a consultant or advisor?
Advisors give input. Consultants write reports. We do the work — directly beside the founder. Every engagement ends with something built, hired, launched, or systematized. Not a deck. Not a list of recommendations.
What stage of founder is this for?
Idea-stage, pre-seed, and early seed. If you're overwhelmed, carrying too many company functions, or watching one unresolved problem slow everything down — this is built for you.
What does the free diagnosis actually look like?
You fill out the intake form describing what's stuck. We review it and come back with a clear picture of the bottleneck, the first sprint we'd run, and the engagement model that fits — at no cost, no pressure.
How long do engagements typically run?
Most sprints are 2–6 weeks, focused on one bottleneck. Monthly engagements typically run 4–8 weeks. We don't push for longer contracts — the goal is to unblock you fast, transfer what we built, and leave you self-sufficient.
Do I need a pitch deck to apply?
No. A short description of what's stuck is all we need. We're not evaluating your company — we're evaluating whether we can actually help you move.
Can you help with multiple areas at once?
We work on one bottleneck at a time. Trying to fix everything simultaneously is part of what creates the stuck-ness most founders experience. Focus is the whole model.
